question: Which is larger, Ganymede or Mercury?
gold: Ganymede#Quick facts|Ganymede#main; Mercury#Quick facts|Mercury#Notable facts
answer: Ganymede, with a diameter of 5,268 km against 4,879 km for Mercury, although it has only about half of Mercury's mass.

[Q14]
type: multi-hop
question: Which is hotter, Venus or Mercury, and why?
gold: Venus#Atmosphere|Venus#Quick facts; Mercury#Temperature and exosphere|Mercury#Quick facts
answer: Venus, at about 465 °C, because its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat. Mercury reaches about 430 °C by day, but it has almost no atmosphere to hold the heat.

[Q15]
type: multi-hop
question: Which spacecraft discovered the rings of Jupiter and the volcanoes of Io?
gold: Jupiter#Magnetic field and rings|Jupiter#Exploration; Io#Exploration
answer: Voyager 1, in 1979.

[Q16]

gold: Neptune#Quick facts; Pluto#Quick facts
answer: Pluto, at 5.9 billion km on average against 4.5 billion km for Neptune, about 1.4 billion km farther.

[Q17]
type: trap
question: Is Mercury the hottest planet in the Solar System?
gold: Mercury#Temperature and exosphere|Venus#Atmosphere|Venus#main
answer: No. Venus is hotter, because its thick atmosphere traps heat, although Mercury is closer to the Sun.

[Q18]
type: trap
question: Does the far side of the Moon never receive sunlight?
gold: Moon#Surface features
answer: No. The far side receives as much sunlight as the near side, but it can never be seen from Earth.

[Q19]
type: trap
question: Is Pluto still classified as a planet?
gold: Pluto#Discovery and reclassification|Pluto#main
answer: No. It was reclassified as a dwarf planet by the International Astronomical Union on 24 August 2006.

[Q20]
type: trap
question: Is the asteroid belt dangerous for spacecraft crossing it?
gold: Asteroid_belt#Is the belt dangerous to spacecraft?
answer: No. The asteroids are spread over a huge volume, and the average distance between two large asteroids is hundreds of thousands of kilometres.
